How to order

No mystery. No checkout roulette.

Every custom project starts with a conversation. Tell us what you want, and we’ll work out the art, material, price, and timing before anything is made.

  1. 01

    Send the idea

    Use the binder request form, attach your reference images, and tell us what matters most.

  2. 02

    Review the plan

    We’ll follow up with any questions, recommend the best approach, and send your quote.

  3. 03

    Approve the artwork

    You’ll see the design before production. We don’t make it until the details are right.

  4. 04

    We make the thing

    Once approved, your project moves into production and we’ll keep you posted.
